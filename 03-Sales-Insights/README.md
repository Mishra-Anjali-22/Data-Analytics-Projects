# Sales Insights

**Tools:** Power BI

One-page sales dashboard for a hardware business selling across multiple Indian markets, built so a sales director can see revenue, volume and top performers at a glance and filter by date.

## Dashboard contents

- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Total Sales Quantity
- **Revenue by Market** and **Sales Quantity by Market** — which cities drive the business
- **Top 5 Customers** and **Top 5 Products** by revenue
- **Revenue Trend** over time
- **Date slicers** for year and month
